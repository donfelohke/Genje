## Reproducibility and rights

A recording documents an occurrence but does not prove that the source, environment, routing or instructions can reproduce it. Identify external dependencies and distinguish live improvisation from a repeatable composition or performance system.

Credit collaborators and upstream projects. Confirm permissions and licence terms for samples, music, images, video, code, recordings and participant contributions. Access to a file is not permission to publish it.

## Evidence discipline

Keep cited facts, interpretation, testimony, memory and creative intention distinct. Retain context and uncertainty; do not flatten people or traditions into generic datasets. Never publish private participant details or credentials.
