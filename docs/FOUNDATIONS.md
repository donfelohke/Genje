# GENJE — Foundations

**Current identity:** GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE  
**Creative principle:** Penye NIA, Pana Njia.  
**Domain:** Genge · AfroTech · livecoding · sound · movement · choreography · choreohacking.

## Foundational principles

1. Culture and computation remain connected to the people and contexts that give them meaning.
2. The human remains the author; tools and systems support, but do not replace, human intent and responsibility.
3. Sound, code, timing, gesture and spatial movement may be explored as related creative structures without claiming they are identical.
4. Credit collaborators, source works, performers, researchers and upstream tools accurately.
5. Record consent and distribution permissions for recordings and identifiable participants.
6. Distinguish research notes, rehearsal, improvisation, prototype, performance and released work.
7. Reproducibility is a claim to evidence, not an aesthetic aspiration. Document versions, environment and observed results when claiming it.
8. Preserve uncertainty and provenance; do not convert a brand interpretation into linguistic fact.

## Creative field

GENJE holds Genge-rooted aesthetics and sound, AfroTech, algorithmic music, livecoding, audiovisual experimentation, dance, choreography, Laban-informed movement research and choreohacking. These are connected strands of a living practice, not proof that every tool or method is currently implemented.

## Naming and meaning

**“The Fire of the Formidable”** is the adopted creative interpretation of GENJE ZA WARENA. It expresses purpose, energy, discipline and formidable follow-through. It is not asserted as a universal translation or verified etymology.

Meanings and social usage of *Genje*, *Warena*, *Za* and *Genjeness* remain research questions unless supported by contextual evidence. Record dialect, speaker/community context, source, date and competing interpretations. Keep authorial intent distinct from attested usage.
