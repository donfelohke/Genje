# GENJE — Foundations

## Principles

1. Culture and computation stay tied to the people who give them meaning  
2. Credit collaborators; record consent for recordings  
3. Distinguish session notes from published works  
4. Reproducibility for live code is aspirational — document versions and seeds when possible  

## Naming

Linguistic readings of *Genje*, *Warena*, *Genjeness* are **research questions** unless independently supported. Brand intent ≠ etymology proof.

## Relation to pillars
