## Meaning and purpose

GENJE explores how culture is lived, interpreted and created through sound, movement, performance, code and collective expression. It treats technology as a medium and the human creator as the author.

Its practice may include Genge, AfroTech, Genge Algo, Boomba Sounds & Riddims, algorithmic music, audiovisual performance, dance, choreography, improvisation, Laban-informed movement study and choreohacking.

## Creative philosophy

- African creativity is contemporary, diverse, experimental and future-facing.
- Cultural sources and collaborators deserve attribution.
- Movement and sound are authored forms, not merely data streams.
- Computation should expand creative agency, not erase the performer.
- Livecoding and generative systems are methods, not proof of a finished release.
- Sensing or capture involving people must be optional, informed, consent-based and proportionate.
- Do not infer rights or permission from public availability.
