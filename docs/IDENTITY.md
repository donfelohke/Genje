# GENJE identity

**GENJE** names the creative-cultural vector of the FELOH practice.

## GENJE ZA WARENA

**GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE.**

This is the adopted creative interpretation and motivating identity: purpose, discipline, creative energy and formidable follow-through. It is an authored identity frame, not a verified universal translation or etymological claim.

**Penye NIA, Pana Njia.**

GENJE is the cultural and creative field: Genge-rooted aesthetics, AfroTech, livecoding, movement, choreohacking and embodied practice. WARENA remains the distinct systems-engineering identity. Their relationship does not make GENJE a WARENA product or division.
