# GENJE identity

**GENJE** names the creative-cultural vector of the practice.

**GENJE ZA WARENA** is used as a unifying motivational identity — purpose, discipline, and formidable follow-through — without becoming a separate till or product line.

## Affirmations

- Culture and computation can stay connected to the people who give them meaning.  
- Movement, sound, and code can share structure (syntax, flow, timing).  
- Authorship stays human.

## Refusals

- Selling GENJE as if it were Locs pricing.  
- Replacing NAKEBUSEN as the public commercial name.  
- Inflating research sketches into “platforms.”
