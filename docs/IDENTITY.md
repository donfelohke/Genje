# GENJE Identity

**GENJE** names the cultural and creative vector of the FELOH practice.

## GENJE ZA WARENA

**GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE.**

This is the adopted creative interpretation and motivating identity: purpose, discipline, creative energy and formidable follow-through. It is an authored identity frame, not a verified universal translation or etymological claim.

**Penye NIA, Pana Njia.**

GENJE is the creative field: Genge-rooted sound and aesthetics, AfroTech, livecoding, audiovisual work, movement, choreography, choreohacking and embodied practice. The human creator remains the author.

## Identity roles

- **FELOH / Don Feloh:** human author, founder and integrated creative practice.
- **NAKEBUSEN:** parent enterprise and only commercial interface — **Beautiful Roots. Sovereign Systems.**
- **WARENA:** distinct systems-engineering identity and technical enablement for NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL.
- **GENJE:** cultural identity, creative expression and embodied practice.
- **IKAI:** reflective-compute research.
- **FELOH Corpus:** shared canon, evidence, terminology and portfolio decisions.
- **Gallery:** visual assets, provenance, permissions and approvals.

GENJE and WARENA are related through creative and infrastructural practice, but remain distinct domains. GENJE is not a WARENA product, company or commercial division.

## Creative character

GENJE connects Genge-rooted rhythm and aesthetics with AfroTech, algorithmic composition, live systems, dance, choreography and movement research. It values cultural specificity, experimentation, craft, improvisation, technical curiosity and accountable documentation.

The visual language should be authored and editorial: rhythm without clutter, material depth without generic luxury, technology without spectacle for its own sake, and cultural references with context. Ghetto-Luxe Dark Academia and Silicon Savannah neo-naturalism are working design references rather than universal cultural categories.

## Affirmations

- Culture and computation stay connected to the people who give them meaning.
- Sound, code, timing and movement can be explored as related creative structures.
- Human authorship and responsibility remain explicit.
- Research, prototypes, live practice and released works are labelled according to evidence.
- Provenance, consent and credit are part of the work, not afterthoughts.

## Refusals

- Selling GENJE as a separate checkout or service division.
- Replacing NAKEBUSEN as the public commercial name.
- Treating an authored brand interpretation as verified etymology.
- Inflating research sketches into released platforms or performances.
- Treating culture, bodies or participants as generic training data.
- Presenting unlicensed or unapproved media as cleared campaign assets.
