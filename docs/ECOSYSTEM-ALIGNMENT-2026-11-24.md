## Identity

GENJE is Don Feloh's embodied creative practice across Genge-rooted sound and aesthetics, AfroTech, algorithmic music, livecoding, audiovisual experimentation, dance, choreography, movement notation and choreohacking.

**GENJE ZA WARENA** is an authored creative signature describing the relationship between creative expression and technical enablement. It does not make GENJE a WARENA product, subsidiary, commercial checkout or merged organisation.

**Penye NIA, Pana Njia.**

“The Fire of the Formidable” remains a creative interpretation, not a universal translation or established etymology. Treat genje and warena as context-sensitive Sheng expressions and keep artist attribution distinct from dictionary or historical claims.

## Creative and ethical standards

Keep the human creator and cultural context central. Credit collaborators and source material. Do not infer permission from public availability. Do not represent a draft, generated reference or research concept as an approved campaign asset or released work. Any capture or sensing involving people must be optional, informed, consent-based and proportionate; no automated identity scoring or covert profiling.

## Maturity and documentation

Label each work LIVE, PROTOTYPE, RESEARCH, CONCEPT, HISTORICAL, SUPERSEDED or UNKNOWN as appropriate. Record actual tools, versions, inputs, outputs, performance conditions, collaborators, rights and validation. A notation study is not a deployed motion-capture system; a source file is not proof of a completed performance.
