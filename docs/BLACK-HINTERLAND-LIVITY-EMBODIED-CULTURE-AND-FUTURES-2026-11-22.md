# Black Hinterland — Living Culture, Embodied Knowledge and Creative Futures

**GENJE / EXPRESSION research note · 22 November 2026 · Status: RESEARCH**  
**Canonical framework:** [FELOH Corpus — BLACK HINTERLAND: BEFORE AFRICA](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/docs/BLACK-HINTERLAND-BEFORE-AFRICA-MASTER-DOSSIER-2026-11-22.md)

## Scope

GENJE investigates living cultural expression: sound, rhythm, music, movement, dance, choreography, craft, oral performance, language and embodied knowledge. Its contribution to BLACK HINTERLAND is not to claim that contemporary Genge or any current creative form descends directly from a prehistoric artefact. It is to document situated living practice and explore how cultural knowledge can be carried, transformed and authored into the future.

## Research principles

- Name the community, language, practitioner, place, period and context.
- Distinguish testimony, historical evidence, scholarly interpretation and artistic response.
- Treat “livity” as a historically situated modern Rastafari-associated term and comparative lens, not a universal ancient African category.
- Do not infer cultural authenticity or direct descent from genomic ancestry or superficial stylistic resemblance.
- Record collaborators, sources, permissions, rights, translation and transformations.
- Respect sacred, restricted and community-held knowledge; access does not equal permission to publish.
- Distinguish notation, movement analysis, recording and cultural interpretation; none is a complete substitute for situated knowledge.

## Creative futures

Research African languages in creative tools; locally controlled archives; ethical sound and movement datasets; open and interoperable notation; livecoding and audiovisual systems; community-led preservation; rights-aware distribution; and new forms of Genge-rooted speculative expression. Reuse and credit existing systems and scholarship before proposing new formats.

Africanfuturism, Afrofuturism, Indigenous Futurisms, Pan-Africanism and solarpunk are related but distinct traditions. GENJE may participate in these conversations without collapsing them into a single aesthetic.
