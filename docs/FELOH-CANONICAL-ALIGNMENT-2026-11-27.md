## SHENG / SHENGE / genje

Keep these layers distinct:
- **Sheng** is the living Kenyan urban linguistic and cultural environment; usage varies by community, generation and context.
- **genje** has documented context-sensitive Sheng senses, including trouble/problems, alongside popular-cultural interpretations associated with a formidable or hardcore crew/quality. Do not present one sense as universal.
- **SHENGE** is retained as a founder-supplied heritage / Sheng-associated expression for “great(-ness)” pending direct speaker attribution, contextual examples and linguistic validation. It is not yet an independently verified dictionary gloss.
- **Genge** is a Kenyan music-cultural lineage informing GENJE's sound and aesthetic. This influence does not prove GENJE is a historical variant, direct translation or etymological derivative of Genge.
- **GENJE** is the authored practice identity; **GENJENESS** is its authored abstract extension.
