# GENJE — Stewardship

## Human authorship

- Founder / human author: Felix Lwova · Don Feloh · @donfelohke · #HisGenjeness.
- Creative and editorial decisions remain human decisions.
- Credit collaborators, performers, researchers, source creators and maintainers according to their actual contributions.

## Repository stewardship

- Repository: [donfelohke/Genje](https://github.com/donfelohke/Genje).
- Default branch: `main`.
- Prefer focused branches and pull requests for substantive changes.
- Keep private collaborator contacts, credentials and personal information out of public Git.
- Link cross-project decisions to the FELOH Corpus.

## Current maturity

The repository is a creative-practice and research documentation base. Individual works must be labelled separately as `LIVE`, `PROTOTYPE`, `RESEARCH`, `CONCEPT`, `HISTORICAL`, `SUPERSEDED` or `UNKNOWN`. This does not assert a released GENJE product suite or a formal governance council.

## Handover

For durable work, record its maintainer, source location, dependencies, rights and consent restrictions, known limitations and the next responsible action. Do not publish private participant information as a handover shortcut.
