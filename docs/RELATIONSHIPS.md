## GENJE and WARENA

**GENJE ZA WARENA — The Fire of the Formidable** is an authored creative identity and motivating frame. It is not a separate company, product, checkout brand or commercial division.

GENJE holds culture, authorship, movement, sound and creative practice. WARENA holds systems engineering and technical enablement. They can inform and support one another without collapsing into one domain or making GENJE a WARENA product.

## Commercial boundary

NAKEBUSEN remains the parent enterprise and only commercial interface. Public service enquiries, bookings and digital-service transactions route through NAKEBUSEN. An enquiry is not a booking. GENJE records creative works, authorship, collaborators, cultural context, provenance and release state; it does not run a commercial checkout.
