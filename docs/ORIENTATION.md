# GENJE — Orientation

**Domain:** Creative technology · Genge · livecoding · movement · choreohacking  
**Identity line:** **GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE**  
**Author:** Felix Lwova · Don Feloh · @donfelohke · #HisGenjeness  
**Maturity:** CREATIVE PRACTICE / RESEARCH; individual works must be labelled by their actual release state.

## Purpose

Hold the creative and embodied strand of the FELOH practice: Genge-rooted sound, AfroTech, live systems, movement notation, choreohacking, performance and collaborator credits.

## Meaning and boundary

**The Fire of the Formidable** is the adopted creative interpretation and campaign identity. It is not presented as a verified universal translation or etymological claim.

GENJE is a cultural and creative practice, not a separate commercial business, checkout or booking surface. Commercial beauty and digital services remain under NAKEBUSEN. WARENA remains the distinct systems-engineering identity powering NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL.

**Penye NIA, Pana Njia.**

## Audience

Collaborators, researchers, audiences and future practitioners of the work — not a booking cart.

## Documentation contract

For each work, record title, date, medium, source files, author and collaborators, cultural context, tools/versions, setup and reproduction notes, licence, release status, performance context and limitations. Distinguish a sketch, rehearsal, research experiment, prototype and released work.
