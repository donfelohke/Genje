# GENJE Asset Provenance

This register standard applies to campaign imagery, photographs, illustrations, sound, samples, video, code, notation, performance recordings and collaborative material.

## Required record

For each non-trivial third-party, generated or collaborative asset, record:

- Asset title and type.
- Creator / author and rights holder, where known.
- Original source location and stable identifier.
- Date acquired or created.
- Licence name/version or written permission basis.
- Required attribution and notices.
- Whether modification, adaptation, commercial use and redistribution are permitted.
- Consent for identifiable people and recorded participants.
- Intended project, audience, medium and use.
- Restrictions, expiry or withdrawal conditions.
- Review/approval state, reviewer and date.
- Local path or Gallery asset ID, if retained.
- Unresolved rights, attribution or consent questions.

## Review states

Use explicit states such as `REFERENCE`, `DRAFT`, `REVIEW REQUIRED`, `RIGHTS CLEARED`, `APPROVED`, `RESTRICTED`, `REJECTED` or `WITHDRAWN`. A brief, upload or public URL does not itself approve an asset.

## Campaign source set

The GENJE ZA WARENA launch brief refers to one authentic founder portrait and six supporting source images. This describes the intended source set; it does not establish that all seven files are present, cleared or approved. Review each original individually in [Gallery](https://github.com/donfelohke/Gallery) before using it in final artwork.

## Rules

- Public availability is not permission to reuse.
- Do not commit material when rights or consent are unclear; record the gap and resolve it before publication.
- Do not present generated or illustrative imagery as documentary evidence.
- Do not imply a real client result, endorsement or collaboration without verifiable evidence and appropriate approval.
- Respect takedown, retention and deletion requests consistent with applicable rights and agreements.
