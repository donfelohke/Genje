# GENJE Asset Provenance

For each non-trivial third-party or collaborative asset, record:

- Asset title and type.
- Author / rights holder, where known.
- Source location.
- Licence or permission basis and version.
- Required attribution.
- Whether modification is permitted.
- Consent and distribution restrictions for recordings or identifiable participants.
- Local file path or stable identifier, if retained.
- Review date and any unresolved rights question.

Do not assume that public availability means an asset is free to reuse. Do not commit material when rights or consent are unclear; record the gap and resolve it before publication.
