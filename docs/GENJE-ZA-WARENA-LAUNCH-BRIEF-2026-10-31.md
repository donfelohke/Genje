# GENJE ZA WARENA — Website Launch Creative Brief

**Date:** 2026-10-31  
**Status:** Creative direction / campaign brief — not an approved final artwork  
**Identity:** GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE  
**Human founder / author:** Felix Lwova · Don Feloh · #HisGenjeness · @donfelohke  
**Destination:** https://donfelohke.github.io/

## 1. Campaign purpose

Introduce the GENJE ZA WARENA creative identity and direct audiences to the public website. The campaign should communicate a coherent authored practice—Genge, AfroTech, livecoding and movement—while making the human creator and the wider NAKEBUSEN ecosystem legible.

The poster is a forward path to the website, not a replacement for it. The current website is the governing public reference for live messaging, project hierarchy and destination. This brief does not authorize changes to the website repository.

## 2. Message architecture

- **NAKEBUSEN:** Beautiful Roots. Sovereign Systems.
- **Supporting proposition:** Craft, technology and culture rooted in African ingenuity.
- **GENJE ZA WARENA:** The Fire of the Formidable.
- **Creative principle:** Penye NIA, Pana Njia.
- **Creative field:** Genge · AfroTech · Livecoding · Movement.
- **Authorship:** The human remains the author.

“The Fire of the Formidable” is the adopted creative interpretation, not a verified universal translation or etymological statement. Use clear English as the structural layer; Kiswahili and Sheng may add natural voice where they strengthen meaning. Avoid forced slang, invented translations and dense slogan stacking.

Do not present GENJE as a separate commercial business, checkout or service division. Commercial beauty and digital services remain under NAKEBUSEN. WARENA remains the distinct systems-engineering identity.

## 3. Image hierarchy and source integrity

The campaign source set is described as seven supplied images plus the website as the eighth governing reference.

1. **Primary portrait:** the authentic founder portrait featuring the yellow cap and blue shirt. It is the dominant human anchor. Preserve recognisability, expression and source character; do not replace it with a generated likeness.
2. **Six supporting images:** each should contribute a distinct motif, texture, material cue, symbol or compositional reference. They are supporting material, not six competing hero images. Their exact contribution must be recorded from the originals during asset review; do not invent visual details.
3. **Website:** the governing reference for current proposition, project boundaries, public message and destination. It is not another image to collage into the artwork.

The brief does not establish that the seven source images are currently committed, rights-cleared or approved. Confirm their presence and individual provenance in Gallery before final artwork approval.

## 4. Composition and reading order

Recommended sequence:

1. GENJE ZA WARENA — campaign identity.
2. Authentic founder portrait — human authorship and recognition.
3. THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE — adopted identity line.
4. Genge · AfroTech · Livecoding · Movement — concise description of the practice.
5. NAKEBUSEN — Beautiful Roots. Sovereign Systems. — parent ecosystem context, subordinate to the creative announcement.
6. Clear website destination and verified social/contact details.

Keep the founder portrait dominant. Supporting images should create rhythm, depth and coherence without reducing legibility. Use a deliberate editorial composition rather than an unstructured collage. The poster must remain readable at mobile display size and when forwarded through messaging apps.

## 5. Visual design direction

Aim for an authored, culturally situated visual language: Genge energy, embodied gesture, African sonic/material references, computational pattern and editorial restraint. Ghetto-Luxe Dark Academia and Silicon Savannah neo-naturalism are working references, not rigid genre labels or claims to represent all African culture.

Prioritise clear hierarchy, strong figure-ground separation, tactile material cues, purposeful negative space and disciplined typography. Avoid generic luxury templates, excessive effects, default Rasta shorthand, neon-AI haze, decorative tech clutter and ornamental symbols without provenance. Do not allow the supporting motifs to overwhelm the human subject or message.

## 6. Authorship, rights and approvals

- Preserve the original founder portrait and its recognisable identity.
- Do not imply generated imagery is documentary evidence.
- Do not fabricate biography, credentials, awards, collaborations, performance history, testimonials, audience size, product availability or software releases.
- Record creator/source, rights holder, licence or permission, attribution, consent, intended use and restrictions for every asset.
- Keep draft, reference, reviewed, approved and released states distinct.
- Obtain explicit approval before marking the final composition or any source asset approved.
