# GENJE ZA WARENA — Website Launch Creative Brief

**Date:** 2026-10-31  
**Status:** Creative direction / campaign brief  
**Identity:** GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE  
**Founder / author:** Felix Lwova · Don Feloh · #HisGenjeness · @donfelohke

## 1. Campaign purpose

Announce and direct audiences to the GENJE ZA WARENA web presence while making the founder, creative identity and wider NAKEBUSEN ecosystem legible.

The poster is a forward path to the website, not a replacement for the website. Use the website as the governing reference for current messaging, project hierarchy and public destination.

## 2. Message architecture

- **NAKEBUSEN:** Beautiful Roots. Sovereign Systems.
- **Supporting proposition:** Craft, technology and culture rooted in African ingenuity.
- **GENJE ZA WARENA:** The Fire of the Formidable.
- **Creative principle:** Penye NIA, Pana Njia.
- **Human authorship:** The human remains the author.

Do not present GENJE as a separate commercial business, checkout or service division. Commercial beauty and digital services remain under NAKEBUSEN. WARENA remains the distinct systems-engineering identity.

“The Fire of the Formidable” is the adopted creative interpretation for this identity, not a verified universal translation or etymological statement.

## 3. Image hierarchy

The campaign source set consists of seven supplied images plus the website as the eighth governing reference.

1. **Primary portrait:** the authentic founder portrait featuring the yellow cap and blue shirt. Keep the person recognisable and preserve the source portrait's identity and character. This is the dominant human anchor.
2. **Six supporting images:** use each as a source of a distinct supporting motif, texture, material cue, symbol or compositional reference. Do not treat them as six competing hero images; do not flatten the design into a collage.
3. **Website reference:** use the current website as the authority for message, naming, project boundaries, visual identity and destination. The poster must point forward to it.

Do not invent details about the six supporting images. Their exact visual contribution must be recorded from the original source images during asset review.

## 4. Composition and reading order

Recommended reading sequence:

1. GENJE ZA WARENA — campaign identity.
2. Founder portrait — human authorship and recognition.
3. The Fire of the Formidable — adopted identity line.
4. A concise statement of the creative field: Genge · AfroTech · Livecoding · Movement.
5. NAKEBUSEN — Beautiful Roots. Sovereign Systems. as the parent ecosystem context.
6. Clear website destination and verified social/contact details.

Keep the founder portrait dominant. Supporting imagery should create depth, rhythm and visual coherence without reducing legibility or competing with the primary subject. The final piece should feel authored and intentional rather than like a template collage.

## 5. Language

Use clear English as the structural layer. Kiswahili and Sheng can add natural cultural voice where they strengthen meaning. Preserve the exact phrase **Penye NIA, Pana Njia**. Avoid forced slang, invented translations, unsupported etymology and dense slogan stacking.

## 6. Authorship and asset integrity

- Preserve the original founder portrait; do not replace it with a generated likeness.
- Do not imply that generated or illustrative images are documentary evidence.
- Do not fabricate awards, credentials, collaborations, event history, audience size or released software.
- Record the source, creator, rights, consent and intended use for every image.
- Keep draft, reference, approved and released states distinct.
- The seven original images must be present and individually reviewed before any derivative is approved.

## 7. Approval checklist

- [ ] Founder portrait is authentic, recognisable and dominant.
- [ ] All six supporting images have individually documented contributions.
- [ ] Website messaging and project hierarchy are reflected accurately.
- [ ] GENJE and WARENA remain distinct.
- [ ] NAKEBUSEN remains the only commercial interface.
- [ ] Website destination and contact/social details are verified against the live reference.
- [ ] Copy is legible at mobile poster size.
- [ ] Rights, consent, provenance and approvals are recorded in Gallery.
- [ ] No unsupported product, service, biography or performance claims.
