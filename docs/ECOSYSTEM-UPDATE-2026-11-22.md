# GENJE living practice and creative systems update — 2026-11-22

**Domain:** EXPRESSION. **Status:** Creative and cultural research; no new release or fieldwork is asserted.

## Scope

GENJE documents situated Genge-rooted sound, rhythm, music, movement, dance, choreography, craft, oral performance, livecoding and embodied knowledge. Contemporary forms must not be represented as direct descendants of prehistoric artefacts without evidence.

## Research and practice method

- Identify community, language, practitioner, place, period and context.
- Distinguish primary testimony, historical evidence, scholarship, artist interpretation and the repository author's own reading.
- Preserve creator and collaborator credit, rights, consent, source locators and transformation history.
- Treat notation, movement analysis, recordings and cultural interpretation as complementary rather than interchangeable.
- For livecoding or movement studies, record exact tools/versions, timebase, input/output paths, synchronisation, latency method, observed results and failure cases.
- Reuse existing notation, protocols, tools and scholarship before designing new formats; credit upstream creators.

## IKAI relationship

IKAI owns reflective-computing and participant-facing mirror research. GENJE contributes authored movement, choreography, livecoding and participant-led creative context. Optional sensing remains a research proposal until a bounded, consent-based experiment is actually tested. No motion-capture implementation or biometric inference is claimed.

## Maturity and release

A patch, score, document or repository is not proof of a reproducible performance or public release. Record the work, contributors, rights, consent, context, tool versions and human approval before publication.
