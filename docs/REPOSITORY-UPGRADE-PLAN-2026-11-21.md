# Repository upgrade plan — 2026-11-21

**Repository:** `Genje`  
**Primary role:** EXPRESSION — Genge-rooted sound, movement, livecoding, choreography and embodied creative practice.

## Creative and cultural principles

GENJE is an authored practice, not a checkout brand or a generic pan-African aesthetic. Preserve cultural specificity, source attribution, collaborator credit, consent and the distinction between an artist's interpretation and a universal linguistic claim.

**ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.** GENJE NODE is a WARENA manifestation, not a fifth domain.

## Upgrade workstreams

1. **Research provenance:** cite interviews, recordings, dictionaries, scholarship and field notes; date access and distinguish attributed interpretation from established usage.
2. **Creative work records:** document concept, contributors, tools, version, performance context, rights, consent, release status and reproducibility.
3. **Livecoding:** keep TidalCycles, SuperCollider, Hydra and other tools as explored components unless a specific working setup and version is evidenced.
4. **Movement/choreohacking:** distinguish notation, score, rehearsal, sensor experiment and performed work; do not imply motion-capture capability without a tested system.
5. **IKAI relationship:** IKAI owns reflective participant experience; GENJE owns authored movement and creative interpretation; WARENA owns infrastructure.
6. **Design system:** retain rhythm, materiality, strong hierarchy and editorial restraint; avoid generic luxury, forced slang, cultural flattening and decorative technology clutter.
7. **Release and archive:** add work only with credits, rights and context; distinguish public release from private study and unfinished experiment.

## Quality gates

No universal etymology claim without adequate sources. No person imagery or recording without appropriate consent and usage rights. No prototype or performance claim without a record of what was actually run or performed.

3. Establish a consistent work-record template.
4. Identify one bounded, testable livecoding/movement study.
