# GENJE — Name Note

**Status:** RESEARCH · canonical identity definition recorded in FELOH Corpus.

GENJE is the cultural and creative field of the FELOH practice, with documented Genge-rooted sound and aesthetics alongside livecoding, audiovisual experimentation, movement, choreography and choreohacking.

**GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE** is the adopted creative identity line. “The Fire of the Formidable” is a founder-adopted creative interpretation, not a verified literal translation or etymology. The relationship to Genge is a stated creative context, not proof of a historical linguistic derivation.

The authoritative exploration is maintained in the [FELOH Corpus — GENJE Name and Identity Exploration](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/docs/research-briefs/genje-name-exploration-2026-11.md).

**Boundary:** GENJE is a creative practice, not a separate company, product catalogue or checkout surface. Commercial enquiries route through NAKEBUSEN.
