# GENJE — Name Note

**Status:** RESEARCH · canonical identity definition recorded in FELOH Corpus.

GENJE is the cultural and creative field of the FELOH practice, with documented Genge-rooted sound and aesthetics alongside livecoding, audiovisual experimentation, movement, choreography and choreohacking.

**GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE** is the adopted creative identity line. “The Fire of the Formidable” is a founder-adopted creative interpretation, not a verified literal translation or etymology.

## Sheng and identity context

*Genje* is a living, context-sensitive Sheng expression. Recorded sources include senses such as trouble or problems, while popular-culture and music-scene usage also associates it with a formidable, hardcore or “hot” crew or quality. Mbogi Genje's own 2021 explanation of its group name—as a “closed fist” set to conquer / a formidable unit—is an attributed artist interpretation, not a universal dictionary definition.

*Genjeness* and **His Genjeness** are creative English–Sheng formations. The English suffix *-ness* forms an abstract noun, while the honorific plays on formal English titles. In this practice they express an authored sense of creative force, formidable presence and cultural confidence. They are identity language, not claims about uniform usage by all Sheng speakers.

Do not flatten these usages into one fixed definition or describe the newer popular-culture senses as a complete, universal replacement for older senses. Keep community and artist interpretations attributed and contextual.

The WARENA connection is cultural resonance and shared creative practice, not a claim that *genje* and *warena* share a historical etymology.
