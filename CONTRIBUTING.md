# Contributing to GENJE

Contributions should strengthen the creative record without obscuring authorship, cultural context, rights or the actual maturity of the work.

## Before proposing a contribution

Describe:
- Purpose, creative intent and relevant cultural context.
- Human author(s), collaborators, roles and credits.
- Whether the contribution is research, concept, prototype, live practice, historical record or release.
- Technical tools, versions, hardware, dependencies and source references where relevant.
- Any third-party music, samples, images, video, code, recordings or other materials.
- Consent, privacy, licensing and distribution restrictions.

## Media and performance material

- Confirm rights or permissions for included material before publication.
- Credit collaborators, performers, researchers and upstream projects accurately.
- Respect participant consent and agreed distribution limits.
- Do not publish private rehearsal footage, messages, contact details, credentials or identifiable participant information without appropriate permission.
- Do not present generated or illustrative material as documentary evidence.
- Do not imply a performance, collaboration, workshop, client result or release exists without a source record.

## Reproducibility and validation

Where reproducibility matters, document source commit, tool/runtime versions, environment, hardware, routing, dependencies, setup and execution steps. State what was actually tested, on which environment, what was observed and what remains unverified. A recording documents an occurrence; it does not by itself prove that the work can be reproduced.

## Pull requests

Use a focused branch and explain:
1. What changed and why.
2. Which authorship, identity or design decisions it reflects.
3. Rights/provenance review completed or still outstanding.
4. Validation performed and its result.
5. Known limitations and follow-up work.

Do not turn a proposal into a claim of implementation through repetition.
