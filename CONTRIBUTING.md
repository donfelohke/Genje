# Contributing to GENJE

For each contribution, describe its purpose, authorship, cultural context, technical requirements, and current maturity.

Before publishing media or performance material:
- Confirm you have the rights or permissions required for included music, samples, images, video, code, and recordings.
- Credit collaborators and upstream projects.
- Respect participant consent and agreed distribution limits.
- Remove private information and credentials from source, screenshots, logs, and recordings.
- Document tools, versions, routing, and steps needed to revisit the work where reproducibility is relevant.

Pull requests should state what was tested, on which environment, what was observed, and what remains unverified. Do not describe a performance as reproducible solely because a recording exists.
