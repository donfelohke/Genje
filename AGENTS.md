# GENJE contributor and agent instructions

## Scope and authority
- Read `README.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, and relevant creative/performance records before proposing changes.
- Consult the [GENJE Choreohacker skill](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/skills/genje-choreohacker/SKILL.md) as guidance; the canonical shared terminology remains in the FELOH Corpus.

## Creative and technical integrity
- Preserve human authorship, attribution, collaborators' contributions, cultural context, and source provenance.
- Distinguish documented fact, interpretation, concept, prototype, performance, and historical material.
- Confirm rights, consent, and intended use before adding recordings, photographs, source works, participant information, or third-party media.
- Do not invent credits, event participation, performance outcomes, or permissions.
- Keep sensitive participant details and private recordings out of public Git history.

## Change control
- Inspect existing source and records before adding new material.
- Use a focused branch and pull request; describe the creative/technical intent, materials and rights involved, checks performed, and limitations.
- Preserve reversible source material and document reproduction steps when applicable.
- Never claim a performance, build, or deployment was tested unless evidence supports the claim.
