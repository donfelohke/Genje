# GENJE Governance

GENJE is responsible for creative technology, Genge, livecoding, performance, movement, and choreohacking within the FELOH ecosystem.

## Principles

- Credit authors, collaborators, performers, researchers, and source works accurately.
- Preserve cultural context and distinguish interpretation from documented fact.
- Respect consent, privacy, licensing, and agreed uses of recordings and media.
- Prefer open, inspectable and revisitable technical processes where appropriate.
- Support contributor stewardship and succession; do not imply permanent founder control.
- Keep creative choices and technical claims distinguishable.

## Change process

Use focused branches and pull requests for substantive changes. Describe the creative/technical intent, materials and rights involved, validation performed, and known limitations. Link ecosystem-level decisions to the FELOH Corpus.

Status labels: `LIVE`, `PROTOTYPE`, `RESEARCH`, `CONCEPT`, `HISTORICAL`, `SUPERSEDED`, `UNKNOWN`.

This file records intended governance; it does not assert that a formal council, review rota, or repository protection rule is configured.
