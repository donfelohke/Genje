# GENJE Governance

GENJE stewards the cultural and creative practice of the FELOH ecosystem: Genge-rooted sound and aesthetics, AfroTech, livecoding, audiovisual work, movement, choreography, choreohacking and the documentation of authored creative work.

## Governing principles

1. **Human authorship:** the human creator remains responsible for artistic intent, selection, interpretation and release decisions. Tools can assist; they do not become the author by default.
2. **Cultural context:** credit sources and collaborators; distinguish documented fact, testimony, interpretation, memory and creative intention.
3. **Consent and dignity:** respect participants, performers, collaborators and people depicted. Do not publish private material or exceed agreed uses.
4. **Rights and provenance:** record source, creator, licence/permission, attribution and restrictions for music, samples, images, video, code and recordings.
5. **Inspectability:** where relevant, preserve source versions, tools, dependencies, setup and observations so work can be revisited.
6. **Accurate maturity:** distinguish concepts, research, prototypes, live practice, historical work and released artefacts.
7. **Stewardship:** document handover and contributor roles without implying that a formal council, review rota or protection rule exists unless configured.

## Identity and ecosystem boundary

GENJE ZA WARENA — **THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE** — is an authored creative identity and motivating frame, not a verified universal translation and not a separate commercial division. NAKEBUSEN remains the parent enterprise and only commercial interface. WARENA remains the distinct systems-engineering identity. GENJE does not own the commercial booking or checkout journey.

## Maturity labels

Use: `LIVE`, `PROTOTYPE`, `RESEARCH`, `CONCEPT`, `HISTORICAL`, `SUPERSEDED`, `UNKNOWN`. Apply labels to individual works and implementations, not indiscriminately to the whole creative practice.

## Change process

Use focused branches and pull requests for substantive changes. Describe creative and technical intent, authorship, cultural context, assets and rights, maturity, validation performed and known limitations. Link shared identity or ecosystem decisions to the [FELOH Corpus portfolio decision](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/docs/portfolio-decision-2026-10-31.md).

This document records intended governance. It does not assert that a formal committee, review rota, deployment process or repository protection rule is configured.
