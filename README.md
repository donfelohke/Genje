# GENJE — Genge, Livecoding, Movement and Choreohacking

**Author:** Feloh Warena · [@donfelohke](https://x.com/donfelohke) · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/donfelohke/)  
**Pillar:** GLORY · GENJE ZA WARENA — The Fire of the Formidable  
Public creative tags include E-Genge, Algorave, Laban, and choreohack (as stated on X/Instagram).

GENJE / GENJE ZA WARENA is the ecosystem's creative and embodied-practice domain. It brings together Genge and AfroTech cultural practice, livecoding, music and audiovisual performance, movement, and choreohacking.

## Practice principles

- Preserve human authorship, cultural context, credits, and provenance.
- Distinguish original works from tools, samples, recordings, and third-party assets.
- Document the software and equipment needed to revisit a performance.
- Respect collaborator consent, licensing terms, and privacy.
- Treat embodied practice as situated work, not a generic dataset or automated judgement of people.
- Mark work honestly as `LIVE`, `PROTOTYPE`, `RESEARCH`, `CONCEPT`, `HISTORICAL`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `UNKNOWN`.

## Working areas

- Livecoding compositions and source.
- Genge and AfroTech research, performance notes, and cultural context.
- Audio/visual systems and performance routing.
- Movement, choreohacking, and embodied research.
- Workshops, educational material, and rehearsal/performance records.
- Rights, credits, accessibility and consent notes.

## Knowledge base and reproducibility

Start with the [creative practice knowledge-base index](docs/knowledge-base-index.md). Where relevant, record source commit, tool versions, runtime/environment, hardware, audio/visual routing, external media dependencies, performance instructions, and known differences between the original and reproduced result. A recording alone does not prove that the source is reproducible.

## Canonical architecture update — 2026-10-26

**GENJE ZA WARENA — THE FIRE OF THE FORMIDABLE** is the current cultural identity and motivating idea associated with this creative domain and replaces the earlier NIA naming frame. It is not a separate commercial division. GENJE practice includes Genge/AfroTech, live coding, music and audiovisual performance, Laban-related movement research and choreohacking.

Keep the distinctions clear: FELOH is the human author and integrated practice; NAKEBUSEN is the enterprise and sole commercial interface; WARENA is the technical identity of NAKEBUSEN DIGITAL; IKAI Mirror is a reflective-computing research project. Preserve cultural context, authorship, consent and provenance. Proposed meanings of Genje, Za, Warena and Genjeness must be distinguished from documented linguistic evidence and reviewed in context.

See the [canonical architecture and decision register](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/docs/canonical-architecture-2026-10-26.md).
